# 🗑️ X-eleter Lite - Delete Posts/Replies & Remove Retweets FOR FREE!

A lightweight, privacy-focused Tampermonkey userscript that automates the deletion of your tweets/replies and the removal of your retweets on X (formerly Twitter). 

Unlike third-party services that require your login credentials or expensive API access, **X-eleter Lite runs 100% locally in your browser**. It simply simulates human UI clicks to safely clean up your profile.

## ✨ Features

- **🗑️ Smart Deletion**: Automatically finds and deletes your original tweets and replies.
- **🔄 Retweet Removal**: Scans your profile and safely "Undoes" your retweets and quotes.
- **🧠 Intelligent Filtering**: Skips posts you didn't make and ignores context tweets, saving massive amounts of time.
- **📜 Auto-Scroll & Rate-Limit Protection**: Automatically scrolls down to load older tweets and includes built-in delays to prevent X's automation detection.
- **🖱️ Draggable UI**: A sleek, dark-mode control panel that you can drag anywhere on your screen.
- **💻 Live Console**: Real-time logging of every action so you know exactly what the script is doing.

## 🚀 Installation - Tampermonkey / Greasyfork

1. Install the [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) extension for your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
2. Once done just install my script directly from Greasyfork:  
   👉 **[Click here to navigate to Greasyfork where I've hosted my script](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/581283-x-eleter-lite)**
3. You may need to enable the script from the Tampermonkey extension from the browser, just click on the extension after you installed the script and there should be a toggle to enable the script.
4. Navigate to your X.com profile and look for the floating control panel on the right side of your screen!

## 🌐 Installation - Browser Extension (Work in progress)
1. Download the latest release [here](https://github.com/hidaaanx/x-eleter-lite/releases/tag/v2.1)
2. Open Browser Settings -> Extensions -> Manage Extensions
3. Load Unpacked -> Select X-eleter Lite folder (Not the ZIP file)

## 📖 How to Use

1. **To Delete Tweets/Replies**: Go to your profile and click the **"Replies"** tab. Click the **"Start Deleting"** button on the panel.
2. **To Remove Retweets**: Go to your profile and click the **"Posts"** tab. Click the **"Remove Retweets"** button on the panel.
3. The tool will automatically scan, click, and scroll. You can minimize the panel or drag it out of the way while it works.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

- **Use at your own risk.** This tool simulates human clicks. While it includes delays to avoid rate limits, aggressive use may temporarily limit your account's ability to post or interact.
- **Only run this on YOUR OWN profile.** 
- Actions are **permanent** and cannot be undone. 
- This script does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. It runs entirely in your browser.
